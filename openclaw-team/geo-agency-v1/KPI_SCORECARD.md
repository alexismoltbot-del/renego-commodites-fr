# KPI Scorecard

KPIs simples, lisibles, utiles. Pas de vanity metrics en V1.

## Delivery

- `time_to_first_pack_hours`
  - cible: `<= 8h`
- `priority_urls_covered`
  - cible: `5/5`
- `deliverables_completed`
  - cible: `6/6` hors execution

## Content readiness

- `pages_with_direct_answer_block`
  - cible: `>= 4/5`
- `pages_with_author_date_source`
  - cible: `>= 4/5`
- `citation_ready_blocks_created`
  - cible: `>= 9`

## Technical readiness

- `critical_indexation_issues_identified`
  - cible: `all found or explicit none`
- `high_priority_tech_tickets_written`
  - cible: `>= 5`
- `llms_txt_ready`
  - cible: `yes`

## Safety

- `qa_p0_open`
  - cible: `0`
- `risky_actions_without_human_approval`
  - cible: `0`
- `claims_without_source_or_date`
  - cible: `0`

## Weekly learning KPI

- `reusable_patterns_added_to_templates`
  - cible: `>= 1 per run`

Le score le plus important reste simple:

`Can we hand a defensible client pack to a human operator in 2 days?`
